# CROWN WING PARROTS — UK SEO + CONTENT + TECHNICAL SEARCH FOUNDATION

You are working on the CrownWing Parrots website.

Your objective is to transform the website into a **high-quality UK-focused parrot buying resource and commercial website**, not a collection of thin SEO pages.

The primary market is the **United Kingdom**.

The website must serve two purposes simultaneously:

1. Help genuine UK buyers make informed decisions about owning and purchasing parrots.
2. Make CrownWing Parrots highly discoverable for relevant UK commercial and informational searches.

Do NOT create mass-produced SEO content, keyword-stuffed pages, doorway pages, or near-duplicate species/location pages.

Every indexable page must provide a clear reason for existing.

---

# 1. CORE SEO PRINCIPLE

Build the site according to Google's people-first content principles.

The content must:

- Be written primarily for human bird buyers.
- Demonstrate genuine knowledge of parrots.
- Answer real buyer questions.
- Provide useful original information.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Avoid repetitive paragraphs between species pages.
- Avoid automatically generating hundreds of almost-identical pages.
- Avoid creating pages solely because a keyword exists.
- Avoid making unsupported claims.
- Avoid fake reviews, fake experience, fake awards or fake expertise.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as “best parrot breeder in the UK” unless independently substantiated.
- Never invent prices, availability, certifications, licences, veterinary claims, delivery promises or legal permissions.

Google specifically recommends substantial, complete and original information that provides value beyond what is already obvious or copied from other websites.

Reference:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

Create links to the official government guidance rather than copying large sections of it.

# 17. TECHNICAL SEO — CRAWLABILITY

Audit the entire application.

Googlebot must be able to discover important pages through normal crawlable HTML links.

Ensure:

- Important pages use standard `<a href="">` links.
- No important page depends exclusively on JavaScript click handlers.
- Navigation is crawlable.
- Breadcrumbs are crawlable.
- Product/category links are crawlable.
- Internal links return HTTP 200.
- No important page is blocked by robots.txt.
- No important page accidentally contains `noindex`.
- Canonicals point to the intended canonical URL.
- HTTPS is enforced.
- One canonical hostname is selected.
- HTTP redirects to HTTPS.
- www/non-www redirects consistently.
- Trailing slash behavior is consistent.
- Duplicate URL variants are eliminated.
- Query parameters do not create uncontrolled indexable duplicates.
- Search/filter URLs are controlled.
- Empty category pages are not indexable.
- Pagination is crawlable where needed.
- Mobile rendering contains the same important content and links.
- Server errors are fixed.
- Redirect chains are minimized.
- 404 pages are handled correctly.

Google specifically recommends crawlable URLs, appropriate robots.txt configuration, and accurate sitemaps when diagnosing crawl issues.

# 18. CANONICAL URL SYSTEM

Every indexable page must have a correct canonical.

Example:

<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.crownwingparrots.co.uk/parrots-for-sale-uk/" />

Use absolute URLs.

Never canonicalize every species page to the homepage.

Never canonicalize genuinely unique pages to another species.

Audit:

- HTTP/HTTPS
- www/non-www
- trailing slash
- uppercase/lowercase
- query strings
- filters
- sorting URLs
- duplicate routes
- old URLs
- parameter URLs

Google notes that canonicalization depends on both technical signals and content similarity; pages that are substantially different should not simply be collapsed into one canonical cluster.

# 2. CLASSIFY EVERY PAGE

Every page MUST receive exactly one primary classification.

## A — KEEP & STRENGTHEN

Use when:

- The page has genuine search value.
- The topic is relevant to CrownWing.
- The content is useful.
- The page has a clear search intent.
- It deserves to be indexed.
- It can become a strong resource with optimization.

Recommended action:

KEEP + IMPROVE

---

## B — EXPAND

Use when:

- The topic is valuable.
- The URL should exist.
- The page currently lacks sufficient depth.
- The content does not fully answer the user's likely questions.
- The page has commercial or informational potential.

Recommended action:

KEEP URL + SUBSTANTIALLY EXPAND

Do not pad the page with generic paragraphs.

Add information that genuinely answers buyer questions.

---

## C — MERGE

Use when:

- Two or more pages target essentially the same search intent.
- Pages contain significant overlapping content.
- The pages would be stronger as one comprehensive resource.
- Multiple pages are competing against each other.

Recommended action:

Select the strongest URL.

Combine the useful content.

Redirect weaker/duplicate URLs to the stronger page.

Do not merge pages merely because they share a keyword.

---

## D — NOINDEX / REMOVE

Use when:

- The page has little independent search value.
- The content is extremely thin.
- The page is an internal utility page.
- The page is duplicate content.
- The page is obsolete.
- The page exists primarily because of a keyword variation.
- The page cannot reasonably be made useful.
- The page should not appear in Google.

Recommended action:

Determine whether the correct solution is:

- `noindex`
- removal
- replacement with a useful page

Do NOT automatically delete pages.

---

## E — REDIRECT

Use when:

- The URL is obsolete.
- The URL is a duplicate.
- A better replacement page already exists.
- The old URL has meaningful historical value or backlinks.
- The intent has been replaced by a stronger URL.

Recommended action:

Implement an appropriate permanent redirect.

Avoid redirect chains.

---

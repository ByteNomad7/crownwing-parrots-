This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: Black-headed and white-bellied caiques.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

Caiques are long-term companions. Published husbandry references give differing estimates, with some citing approximately 30–40 years. Treat this as planning guidance, not a promised lifespan.

02

Diet & nutrition

Offer a varied, balanced diet with suitable formulated food and fresh produce. Use treats sparingly and keep an eye on body condition.

03

Personality & temperament

Often energetic and exploratory. Give them opportunities to climb, investigate and engage with suitable enrichment while respecting each bird’s boundaries.

04

Housing & enrichment

Provide climbing opportunities, safe foot toys and supervised play. Introductions to other birds should be careful and closely managed.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Calls and vocal activity vary. They should not be selected on the assumption that they will be quiet.

06

Is this group right for you?

People who can offer structured activity and supervise an energetic bird’s exploration.

IN THIS GUIDE

This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: Blue-fronted, yellow-naped and double yellow-headed Amazons.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

Amazon parrots can be long-lived, but published figures differ between species and populations. Confirm the species and age, and plan for a long-term relationship with ongoing veterinary care.

02

Diet & nutrition

Provide suitable formulated food and fresh produce, with measured seeds and nuts rather than a seed-only diet. Balance calorie intake with activity and monitor body condition.

03

Personality & temperament

Often animated and confident. Body language matters, and behaviour can change with maturity and breeding condition. Individual temperament should guide any match.

04

Housing & enrichment

Make room for climbing, exercise and robust enrichment. Provide varied perches and opportunities to search for food.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Calls can be loud. Talking ability varies and should never be promised.

06

Is this group right for you?

Owners willing to learn parrot body language and support exercise, nutrition and consistent routines.

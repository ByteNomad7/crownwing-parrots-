IN THIS GUIDE

This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: Budgerigars, ringnecks, lovebirds, parrotlets and other small parrots.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

This category spans very different birds. Published examples include around 7–12 years for budgerigars and 25–30 years for Indian ringnecks. Choose the exact species before planning its lifetime care.

02

Diet & nutrition

Small parrots need a species-appropriate balance of formulated food, fresh vegetables, some fruit and suitable seeds where appropriate. A seed-only diet is not a balanced plan.

03

Personality & temperament

Social, active birds with preferences that vary by species and individual. Small size does not remove the need for companionship, enrichment and gentle handling.

04

Housing & enrichment

Prioritise usable flight space, safe bar spacing and suitable perch diameters. Small birds also need opportunities to exercise and investigate.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Budgies may chatter frequently; other parakeets can have much louder calls. Research the exact species.

06

Is this group right for you?

Owners looking for a smaller bird while still preparing for meaningful daily care.

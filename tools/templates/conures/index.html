This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: Green-cheeked, sun and jenday conures.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

VCA gives a broad average of 20–35 years for conures. Individual species and birds vary, so this is general guidance rather than a guarantee or an exact estimate for every conure.

02

Diet & nutrition

Suitable pellets and varied fresh produce support balanced nutrition. Avoid relying on seed and nut mixes as the main diet; discuss gradual dietary changes with an avian vet.

03

Personality & temperament

Often active and inquisitive, with a strong interest in exploration. Social preferences and handling tolerance vary by bird.

04

Housing & enrichment

Allow room for movement and supervised exercise. Choose appropriate bar spacing and small-parrot-safe toys without hazardous loose parts.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Some conures have piercing calls. A smaller body does not mean a quiet companion.

06

Is this group right for you?

Households ready for an active small parrot with daily enrichment and social interaction.

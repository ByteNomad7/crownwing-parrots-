BEFORE THE FIRST HELLO

Make room for a
remarkable companion.

A good beginning starts before your bird comes home. Use these questions to guide your research.

01 — THE EVERYDAY

Time to be together

Can you provide daily interaction, enrichment and a consistent routine? Think about workdays, holidays and changes ahead.

02 — THE HOME

Space to thrive

Plan a suitable enclosure, safe exercise space and a calm sleeping area. Consider noise and the people sharing your home.

03 — THE LONG VIEW

Care for the years ahead

Research the species’ lifespan and care costs. Identify an avian vet and discuss diet and health needs before making a decision.

Which parrot is best for a first-time owner?

There is no universal best choice. Size alone does not determine care needs. Start by discussing your experience, available time, living space and tolerance for noise, then research the individual bird and species.

Can I see which birds are currently available?

The species collection introduces the groups Crownwing works with. Current birds, photographs, prices and collection arrangements will be confirmed directly when the business contact details are available.

What should I ask before bringing a bird home?

Ask about the bird’s age, origin, diet, health history, temperament and relevant documentation. Discuss your home setup and confirm any species-specific requirements with an appropriate professional.

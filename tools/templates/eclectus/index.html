This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: Eclectus species from New Guinea, nearby islands and north-eastern Australia.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

Animal Diversity Web describes captive life expectations around 23–28 years and an average around 30. Older individuals are reported elsewhere, but exceptional longevity is not a typical-life guarantee.

02

Diet & nutrition

Discuss an Eclectus-specific nutrition plan with an avian vet, including appropriate fresh produce and formulated food. Avoid routine supplements without professional advice.

03

Personality & temperament

Attentive birds with individual preferences for interaction. Avoid assuming that every bird has the same temperament based on sex or appearance.

04

Housing & enrichment

Provide a roomy enclosure, safe exercise and opportunities to forage. A calm-looking bird still needs movement and mental stimulation.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Eclectus can produce loud calls. Individual vocal patterns and response to household activity vary.

06

Is this group right for you?

Owners who will pay close attention to diet, daily routine and individual behaviour.

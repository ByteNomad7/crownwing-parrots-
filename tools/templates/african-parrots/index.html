This is a species-group guide. Individual needs vary. Examples include: African grey, Timneh, Senegal and Meyer’s parrots.

01

Lifespan & long-term planning

African parrots are a diverse group. African greys can be long-lived, while Senegal and Meyer’s parrots have different life expectations. Published figures vary substantially; identify the exact species and plan for decades of care.

02

Diet & nutrition

Use a balanced, species-appropriate feeding plan with suitable formulated food and fresh produce. Discuss calcium and overall nutrition with an avian vet, particularly for African greys.

03

Personality & temperament

Observant and responsive, with individual differences in confidence and sociability. African greys often benefit from predictable routines; smaller African species have their own distinct personalities.

04

Housing & enrichment

Provide room to move, climb and exercise, alongside safe supervised time outside the enclosure. Offer different perch sizes and rotate suitable chew and foraging toys.

05

Noise & vocalisation

Calls and mimicry vary by species and individual. No bird is guaranteed to talk or remain quiet.

06

Is this group right for you?

A household ready to offer consistent interaction, enrichment and a calm routine.

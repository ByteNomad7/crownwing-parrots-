- Verify any bird-specific health, identification, documents, pricing, availability and transport arrangements separately.
- The preview proxy adds a blocking `X-Robots-Tag`; source metadata is indexable, but Google crawlability on a published host has not been verified.
- No publication, Search Console submission, Google indexing, ranking improvement or field Core Web Vitals result is claimed.

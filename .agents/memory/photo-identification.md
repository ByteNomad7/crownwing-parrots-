name: Photo identification
description: Confidence boundaries for classifying uploaded parrot photography.
---

Colour alone is not enough to identify parrots, especially colour mutations and hybrids. Check the bill, facial skin, body proportions and tail against visual references. Prefer broad group names to unsupported exact species or hybrid-lineage claims, and disclose provisional group matches visibly.

**Why:** A blue-coloured parrot produced conflicting image-only identifications. Reference comparison supported a group-level match without establishing its exact species or colour mutation. External image titles corroborate a comparison; they are not proof of identity.

**How to apply:** Investigate conflicting identifications rather than accepting the first guess. Keep unmatched species guides photo-free rather than substituting another species. Do not infer pedigree, ownership, availability or photographic rights from the upload itself.

Do not number the birds.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this when commissioning the commercial and species-content audit.

**How to apply:** Use meaningful species names for visible bird labels and headings, not numbered bird identities. Existing asset filenames and photo-navigation position counts are not bird identities.

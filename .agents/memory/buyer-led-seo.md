name: Buyer-led SEO
description: Owner-directed commercial search intent without copying competitor claims.
---

Commercial SEO should address UK buyer searches, not stop at technical metadata hygiene or replace clear buying language with vague “for enquiry” wording.

**Why:** The owner explicitly supplied a species-sale page and a UK marketplace category page as inspiration for keywords and search intent. Group-level “for sale” language is compatible with the confirmed enquiry-based business model.

**How to apply:** Use commercial pages for buying searches and retain informational intent on species/care guides. Learn from competitor topics and query patterns, but do not copy their content or adopt their prices, reviews, stock, delivery or rearing claims. Do not claim measured search volume or ranking gains without evidence. Exact-species inventory still needs separate confirmation; the competitor’s Hyacinth Macaw listing is not Crownwing stock.
